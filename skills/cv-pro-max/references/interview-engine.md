# Interview Engine

Interview preparation should be generated from the job requirements and candidate evidence, not generic interview lists.

## Question generation

For each high-priority requirement, generate:

1. likely interviewer question;
2. what it tests;
3. candidate evidence;
4. missing evidence;
5. STAR outline;
6. likely follow-up.

## Question categories

### Technical
Derived from explicit stack and architecture requirements.

### Experience
Derived from candidate projects and responsibilities.

### Behavioral
Derived from ownership, collaboration, conflict, prioritization, failure, and leadership signals.

### Gap
Derived from requirements classified as ADJACENT, GAP, or UNKNOWN.

## STAR preparation

For each story:

**Situation**
- context only; avoid unnecessary setup.

**Task**
- what the candidate personally owned.

**Action**
- concrete actions and decisions.

**Result**
- verified outcome.

Never invent a result.

If the result is unknown:
"Result: [QUESTION — verify outcome before interview]."

## Follow-up engine

For each story ask likely follow-ups:
- Why did you choose this approach?
- What alternatives did you consider?
- What did you personally own?
- What went wrong?
- How did you measure success?
- What would you change?
- Who disagreed and how did you handle it?

## Gap preparation

A gap is not a reason to fabricate.

Prepare an honest answer:

1. acknowledge the gap;
2. identify adjacent experience;
3. explain the transferable concept;
4. explain how the candidate would close the gap.

## Interview evidence ledger

Every prepared story should point to a real Career Profile record.
