# ATS

Prefer:
- standard headings
- conventional chronology
- text-based essential content
- exact job terminology when truthful
- clear titles and dates
- readable structure

Avoid:
- keyword stuffing
- hidden keywords
- deceptive formatting
- unnecessary graphics
- critical information trapped in tables when parsing is uncertain

Validate keyword coverage, requirement coverage, title alignment, chronology, section discoverability, and parsing risk.
