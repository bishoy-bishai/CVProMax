# Cover Letter Engine

## Purpose

Explain the fit between the candidate and the role without repeating the CV.

## Structure

### Opening
Name the role and establish the strongest credible connection.

### Proof
Use one or two relevant examples with real evidence.

### Fit
Connect candidate evidence to actual responsibilities and verified company context.

### Closing
Keep it concise and professional.

## Evidence rules

Company-specific statements require a source.

Candidate-specific claims require candidate evidence.

Never invent:
- passion;
- admiration;
- company culture;
- mission alignment;
- product details;
- recent launches;
- personal reasons.

## Personalization hierarchy

Strongest:
1. specific role responsibility;
2. candidate achievement relevant to it;
3. verified company/product context;
4. genuine motivation supplied by the user.

Weakest:
- generic praise;
- copied mission statements;
- empty excitement.

## Quality test

Delete any sentence that could be sent unchanged to ten unrelated companies.


## Evidence Graph Contract

Before drafting, create a claim map:

| Claim | Requirement | Evidence IDs | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | MATCH / ADJACENT / UNKNOWN |

Only MATCH evidence should support direct candidate claims. ADJACENT evidence must preserve the distinction. UNKNOWN evidence becomes a Question Engine item when material.

### Claim Budget

Use 2–4 candidate claims in a normal cover letter. Prefer the strongest evidence rather than repeating the CV.

### Company Context

Company-specific statements must have a verified source. If no source is available, write the letter without company-specific factual claims rather than guessing.

### Final Consistency Gate

Compare every candidate claim against the same evidence used by the tailored CV. The cover letter must not introduce a stronger scope, metric, technology, or ownership claim than the CV.

### Output

Return:
1. Fit thesis.
2. Claim/evidence map.
3. Finished cover letter.
4. Claims intentionally omitted.
5. Truth and consistency validation.
