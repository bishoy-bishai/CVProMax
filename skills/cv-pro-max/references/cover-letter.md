# Cover Letter Engine

## Purpose

Explain the fit between the candidate and the role without repeating the CV.

## Structure

### Opening
Name the role and establish the strongest credible connection.

### Proof
Use one or two relevant examples with real evidence.

### Fit
Connect candidate evidence to actual responsibilities and verified company context.

### Closing
Keep it concise and professional.

## Evidence rules

Company-specific statements require a source.

Candidate-specific claims require candidate evidence.

Never invent:
- passion;
- admiration;
- company culture;
- mission alignment;
- product details;
- recent launches;
- personal reasons.

## Personalization hierarchy

Strongest:
1. specific role responsibility;
2. candidate achievement relevant to it;
3. verified company/product context;
4. genuine motivation supplied by the user.

Weakest:
- generic praise;
- copied mission statements;
- empty excitement.

## Quality test

Delete any sentence that could be sent unchanged to ten unrelated companies.
