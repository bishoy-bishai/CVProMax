# Humanization

Humanization is an editing pass, not a license to change facts.

## Remove

- generic openings;
- inflated adjectives;
- "passionate about" filler;
- repetitive "I am excited" language;
- excessive em-dash chains;
- vague impact claims;
- copied JD language without evidence;
- fake precision;
- corporate buzzword density.

## Preserve

- technical terms;
- exact metrics;
- candidate voice;
- factual nuance;
- appropriate confidence.

## Test

A strong sentence should answer at least one:
- What did the candidate do?
- What did they own?
- What changed?
- Why is it relevant?

If not, shorten or remove it.
