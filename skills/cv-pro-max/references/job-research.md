# Job and Company Research

Research exists to improve application decisions, not to produce a company encyclopedia.

## Research questions

Depending on the user's request:
- What does the company actually do?
- What is this role responsible for?
- What skills appear repeatedly?
- What is known about compensation?
- What is the reported interview process?
- What do employees report?
- What should the candidate emphasize?
- What risks or unknowns remain?

## Source hierarchy

1. Official job/company sources
2. Filings and investor material
3. Official announcements
4. Reputable market/salary sources
5. Employee review sources
6. Community reports

## Salary

Report:
- source;
- date if available;
- geography;
- level;
- base vs total compensation when known;
- range.

If sources disagree, preserve the disagreement.

Never calculate a fake "true salary."

## Reviews

Separate:
- recurring themes;
- isolated comments;
- positive signals;
- negative signals;
- sample limitations.

Do not treat a handful of reviews as company-wide truth.

## Interview reports

Label as:
"Candidate-reported interview question" or "Reported process."

Never claim it is guaranteed.

## Research output

End with:
- verified facts;
- useful signals;
- candidate implications;
- unknowns;
- source list.
