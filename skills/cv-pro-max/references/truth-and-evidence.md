# Truth and Evidence

Truth protection is a core product feature, not a disclaimer.

## Evidence hierarchy

### Tier 1 — Candidate first-party
- current CV
- portfolio
- project documentation
- user's direct answers
- employment records supplied by the user

### Tier 2 — Primary external
- official company job page
- official company website
- filings
- official documentation

### Tier 3 — Secondary
- Glassdoor
- salary aggregators
- reputable recruiting reports
- interview reports

### Tier 4 — Community
- Reddit
- forums
- social posts

Lower tiers can be useful, but should not silently become facts.

## Claim classes

FACT: directly supported.

VERIFIED: externally verified.

INFERENCE: conclusion from evidence.

HYPOTHESIS: plausible but unverified.

UNKNOWN: evidence unavailable.

## Evidence Ledger

Every material claim should be traceable:

| ID | Claim | Source | Class | Confidence | Allowed wording |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E001 | Built shared UI components | Master CV | FACT | High | Direct |
| E002 | Company has X employees | Official source | VERIFIED | High | Direct |
| E003 | Candidate likely worked at senior scope | Several achievements | INFERENCE | Medium | Qualified |

## Hard rules

Never:
- invent a metric;
- turn an estimate into a fact;
- change a title to sound more senior;
- claim a technology from a neighboring skill;
- invent company strategy;
- invent salary information;
- present reported interview questions as guaranteed.

## Missing information

Use:
- UNKNOWN when the fact cannot be established;
- [QUESTION] when asking the candidate could resolve it;
- [SOURCE NEEDED] when external verification is required.

Do not use plausible filler to close a gap.
