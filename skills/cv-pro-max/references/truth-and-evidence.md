# Truth & Evidence

Use an evidence ledger for material claims.

| Claim | Evidence | Class | Confidence | Allowed wording |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Candidate did X | CV/project/source | FACT | High | Direct |
| Candidate likely did Y | Strong context | INFERENCE | Medium | Qualified |
| Candidate may have Z | Partial evidence | HYPOTHESIS | Low | Ask or qualify |
| Z is unknown | No evidence | UNKNOWN | Low | Do not state as fact |

Never invent metrics, responsibilities, technologies, dates, employers, titles, certifications, scope, or company facts.
